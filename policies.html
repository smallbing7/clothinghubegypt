CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Policies & Terms

These general policies explain how Clothing Hub Egypt handles wholesale enquiries, product information and international shipping.

Wholesale notice: Product prices, MOQ, production time and shipping charges may vary by product, quantity and destination. Final commercial terms are confirmed before an order is finalized.

Shipping

We support international export. Shipping availability, carrier, transit time and cost depend on destination, order size, product requirements and current logistics conditions.

Free delivery is advertised for qualifying orders over $200 and remains subject to destination and final shipping terms confirmed by the export team.

Wholesale Orders

Buyers should provide product names, quantities, destination and customization requirements when requesting a quotation. A quotation is not a confirmed order until the commercial terms are agreed.

Custom & Private Label

Custom labels, branding, packaging, fabric and production requirements can be discussed. Custom production may have different MOQs, lead times and payment terms.

Product Information

Images, colours, measurements and specifications may vary slightly between production batches. Confirm final specifications with the export team before placing a bulk order.

Privacy

Information submitted through our enquiry forms or email is used to respond to business enquiries and provide requested product, quotation and shipping information. We do not ask for sensitive payment credentials through the website enquiry form.
