CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Shipping Policy

We arrange international shipping for wholesale clothing orders. Shipping availability, carrier, transit time and cost depend on destination, quantity, product type and order value.

Delivery

Estimated delivery timelines are confirmed before an order is finalized. Customs clearance, public holidays and destination-country delays may affect transit.

Free Delivery

Free delivery may apply to qualifying orders over $200. Eligibility and final shipping terms are confirmed for the destination before payment.

Customs & Duties

Any import duties, taxes or customs charges imposed by the destination country are the buyer's responsibility unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.
