FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Clothing Hub Egypt is focused on wholesale supply, retailers, distributors, brands and private-label buyers.

Can I request a product price?

Yes. Product pages include space for the current price. Prices can be added and updated product-by-product as your catalogue is finalized.

Can I request custom branding?

Yes. Ask about private labels, branding, packaging, fabric requirements and custom production.

Do you ship internationally?

International shipping can be discussed according to destination, quantity, product type and order requirements.

How do I place an inquiry?

Open the product you are interested in and use the WhatsApp or email inquiry option. For general requirements, use the contact form below.

Can I order different products together?

Yes. Send the product names, quantities and destination in your inquiry so the order can be evaluated together.
