CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for international wholesale buyers, retailers, distributors and private-label brands.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Clothing Hub Egypt focuses on wholesale garment supply for retailers, distributors, fashion brands and private-label buyers.

What products do you supply?

Our catalogue covers men's, women's and kids' garments, including T-shirts, polos, shirts, trousers, jeans, cargo, dresses, tops and sets.

Do you offer custom branding and private label?

Yes. Custom labels, branding, packaging and other private-label requirements can be discussed according to your order and production needs.

Can you produce a buyer's own design?

Yes. You can share your reference, tech pack, artwork or design requirements so the production team can review them.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product, fabric, customization and production requirements. Contact us with your target quantity and we will confirm the suitable MOQ.

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Sample requests can be discussed before bulk production. Share the product, quantity and required specifications with the export team.

How do I request a wholesale quotation?

Send your product category, estimated quantity, destination country and customization requirements through the wholesale inquiry form.

Are your prices wholesale prices?

Our catalogue is designed for wholesale sourcing. Final pricing can vary with quantity, product specification, customization, packaging and shipping requirements.

Can you handle large-volume orders?

Yes. We work with buyers looking for larger wholesale quantities. Send your target quantity and required delivery schedule for a production review.

What fabrics and materials are available?

Available fabrics and compositions depend on the selected garment. Tell us your preferred material, weight, finish or quality requirement when requesting a quote.

Can I request specific colors and sizes?

Yes. Color, size breakdown and other specifications can be discussed based on the selected product and production availability.

Do you provide custom packaging?

Custom packaging requirements can be discussed for suitable wholesale and private-label orders, including buyer branding requirements.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on the product, order quantity, customization and production schedule. We confirm an estimated timeline after reviewing your requirements.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Clothing Hub Egypt is focused on worldwide wholesale export. Share your destination country so shipping options can be reviewed.

Can you arrange shipping for my order?

Shipping arrangements depend on order size, destination and shipment type. Include your destination and quantity in your inquiry so the export team can advise you.

How are shipping costs calculated?

Shipping costs can vary by destination, order volume, package dimensions, weight and shipping method. A shipping estimate can be reviewed with your quotation.

Can I visit or verify the production source?

For serious wholesale buyers, production and sourcing details can be discussed with the export team as part of the order process.

How do I check product availability?

Send the product name or catalogue category and your required quantity. Availability can then be reviewed for your order.

Can I mix different products in one wholesale order?

Mixed-product orders may be possible depending on the quantities and production requirements of each category. Send your product list and quantities for review.

What information should I include in my first inquiry?

Include your name, company, country, products you need, estimated quantity, target specifications and any branding or packaging requirements.

How do I contact the wholesale team?

You can use the wholesale inquiry form on this website or email clothinghubegypt4@gmail.com with your requirements.

Do you work with fashion retailers and online brands?

Yes. We can discuss wholesale and private-label requirements for retailers, distributors, fashion brands and online sellers.

Can you help me choose products for my market?

Yes. Tell us your target market, customer segment, product category and expected quantity, and we can discuss suitable catalogue options.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

We review the information you provide and can follow up regarding product specifications, quantities, pricing, customization, production and shipping requirements.
