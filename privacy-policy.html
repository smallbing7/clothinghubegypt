When you contact us or submit an inquiry, you may provide your name, company name, email address, country, phone or WhatsApp details, product requirements, quantity, shipping destination and other information you choose to share.

We may use submitted information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, discuss product availability, process orders, arrange shipping, communicate about customization and provide customer support.

3. Business Communications

If you contact us about wholesale products, we may reply by email or another contact method you provide. We use information only for legitimate business communication related to your request or relationship with us.

If a payment is made through a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed directly by that provider under its own privacy and security practices. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when reasonably necessary to fulfil a requested quotation, payment, production, logistics or delivery service. We do not sell customer information.

8. Data Retention

Business inquiry and transaction information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, order records, accounting, legal obligations and legitimate business administration.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to social networks, payment providers, shipping services or other external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and you should review them when using those services.

10. Children's Information

This website is intended for business and general customer use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for wholesale business purposes.

11. Your Requests

If you believe information we hold about you is inaccurate or you have a privacy-related request, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action where applicable.

12. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when our website, services, legal requirements or business processes change. The latest version published on this page will apply from its publication date.
