Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Clothing Hub Egypt supplies wholesale garments to international buyers. Shipping terms are confirmed for each order based on destination, quantity, product type, packaging, carrier availability and agreed commercial terms.

1. Order Processing

Orders are prepared after the buyer confirms the product specifications, quantities, destination and agreed quotation. Production or preparation time varies by product and order size and will be communicated before the order is finalized.

2. Delivery Times

Estimated transit and delivery timelines are provided before dispatch. Timelines are estimates rather than guaranteed dates because customs clearance, carrier schedules, weather, public holidays and destination-country procedures can affect delivery.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges depend on destination, shipment size, weight, quantity and selected service. The final shipping cost or shipping arrangement will be communicated before payment or order confirmation.

4. Free Delivery

Free delivery may apply to qualifying orders over $200. This offer is subject to destination, shipment method and final confirmation by Clothing Hub Egypt. Orders that do not qualify may incur shipping charges.

5. Customs, Duties & Taxes

International shipments may be subject to import duties, VAT, customs fees or other charges imposed by the destination country. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, these charges are the buyer's responsibility. Buyers should check their local import requirements before ordering.

6. Shipping Address

Buyers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, recipient name and contact information. Delays or additional costs caused by incorrect or incomplete information may be the buyer's responsibility.

7. Split or Partial Shipments

Where commercially agreed, an order may be shipped in more than one shipment. Any applicable additional shipping arrangements will be confirmed with the buyer before dispatch.

8. Delays & Delivery Issues

If a shipment is delayed, held by customs or returned by a carrier, we will communicate the information available to us and work with the buyer on the next appropriate step. Carrier and customs decisions are outside our direct control.

9. Damaged or Incorrect Goods

Please report damaged, defective or incorrect goods promptly after receipt and provide the order details and clear supporting photographs. We will review the issue under the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

10. International Orders

By placing an international order, the buyer is responsible for ensuring that the import of the ordered products is permitted in the destination country and complies with applicable local requirements.

11. Contact

For shipping questions, provide your destination, product category, quantity and required delivery timeline.
