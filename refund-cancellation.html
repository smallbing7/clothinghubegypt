Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This policy applies to wholesale clothing inquiries and orders placed with Clothing Hub Egypt. Because wholesale, production and custom orders can involve made-to-order goods, cancellation and refund eligibility depends on the stage of the order.

1. Before Order Confirmation

Before an order is confirmed, buyers may request changes or cancellation. The team will confirm whether the request can be accepted and whether any third-party or preparation costs have already been incurred.

2. After Confirmation

Once an order enters production, procurement, customization or dispatch preparation, cancellation may be limited. Any approved cancellation and applicable refund will be assessed according to the order status and agreed commercial terms.

3. Custom & Private-Label Orders

Custom-branded, personalized, private-label or made-to-order products may not be eligible for cancellation or return after production begins because they are prepared to the buyer's specifications. Applicable conditions will be communicated before production.

4. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Goods

If goods arrive damaged, defective or materially different from the confirmed order, contact us promptly with the order reference, product details and clear photographs. We will review the claim and determine the appropriate resolution.

5. Returns

Returns for wholesale orders are subject to prior approval. Products must meet the agreed return conditions and may need to be unused and in resalable condition unless the issue concerns damage, defect or an incorrect shipment.

6. Refund Method & Timing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed using the applicable original payment method or another method agreed with the buyer. The time for funds to appear may also depend on the payment provider or bank.

7. Shipping & Customs Costs

Shipping charges, customs duties, taxes and other third-party costs may be non-refundable unless the parties agree otherwise or the issue is attributable to an incorrect or defective shipment.

8. Order Changes

Requests to change quantities, sizes, colors, branding or delivery details should be made before production or dispatch. Changes may affect the quotation and production timeline.

9. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

Email clothinghubegypt4@gmail.com with your name, company, order or quotation reference, product details, reason for the request and supporting information.

10. Review & Resolution

Each request is reviewed against the confirmed order terms and the circumstances of the case. We aim to communicate the outcome and next steps clearly.
