CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Clothing Hub Egypt is focused on wholesale supply for retailers, distributors, fashion brands and private-label buyers.

How do I get the price for a product?

Open the individual product page and use the inquiry options. Current pricing can be added to the product catalogue as each product is finalized.

Can I request custom branding or private label?

Yes. Custom labels, branding, packaging, fabric requirements and production specifications can be discussed with the export team.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Shipping availability and cost depend on destination, order value, quantity and product requirements. The final shipping arrangement is confirmed before the order is finalized.

Is delivery free?

Free delivery is offered on qualifying orders over $200, subject to destination and final shipping terms. Smaller or non-qualifying orders may have shipping charges.

Can I order different products together?

Yes. Send the product names, quantities and destination in one inquiry so the team can review the order together.
