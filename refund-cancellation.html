Refund & Cancellation Policy

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be submitted as early as possible. Orders already in production, customized or dispatched may not be cancellable.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the applicable amount and processing timeline will be confirmed with the buyer. Payment-provider processing times may apply.

Damaged or Incorrect Goods

Report damaged, defective or incorrect goods promptly with clear photographs and order details so the issue can be reviewed.

Custom Orders

Custom-branded or made-to-order products may have different cancellation and refund conditions, which will be agreed before production.
